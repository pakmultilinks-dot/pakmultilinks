# SEO research and content plan — 1 November 2026

## Research basis

The search intent reviewed was wholesale tissue in Lahore, carton versus piece pricing, tissue types, dispenser compatibility and workplace hygiene procurement. These are purchase decisions the catalog can support. No keyword-volume, competitor traffic, ranking or Search Console figures were available; none are invented here.

Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) informed the distinct buying guides, examples, checklists and original comparison tables. Publishing twenty pages alone does not establish authority. Content should be maintained when product specifications or commercial terms change.

Google's [AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says ordinary SEO fundamentals apply to AI features: indexable text, internal links and structured data matching visible content. No special AI schema or additional machine-readable file guarantees inclusion. Quick answers and clear question headings improve usability; they do not guarantee an AI citation.

The [Article documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) informed BlogPosting author, dates and images. The [structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies) require markup to match visible content. Generic commercial FAQs are not presented as guaranteed Google FAQ rich results; see [Google's FAQ changes](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes). Avoid repetitive, low-value pages under the [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

Narrow factual references include [Rose Petal product families](https://rosepetal.com.pk/products/), [Tork's H2 refill example](https://www.torkglobal.com/gb/en/product/paper-hand-towels/refills/multifold-hand-towels/600297), and [FSC label meanings](https://www.us.fsc.org/what-is-fsc/what-do-the-fsc-labels-mean). Their presence is not a claim that Pak Multilinks or all its products hold a certification. Exact prices, stock and shipping promises should come from the current product record or a confirmed quotation.

Article dates represent this release, not invented historical publication dates.
