# Support answers

60 prepared questions are in `src/lib/support-questions.json`, grouped by topic. Fill each `answer` with your approved reply; keep it empty until ready. These are prepared FAQs, not a trained model.

Only filled answers are supplied to the chat assistant. Empty entries are ignored. Customers are not shown a list of suggested questions. Do not include passwords, banking secrets, or private customer information. Commit and deploy the file after updating answers.
